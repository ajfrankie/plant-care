About This Plant

The Monstera Deliciosa, also known as the Swiss Cheese Plant, is a species of flowering plant native to tropical forests of southern Mexico. It's famous for its large, glossy, heart-shaped leaves with natural holes (fenestrations) that develop as the plant matures. This stunning tropical plant can grow quite large indoors and is perfect for adding a jungle vibe to any space.

💡 Pro Care Tips

  • Provide a moss pole or stake for support as the plant grows

  • Rotate plant weekly for even growth on all sides
  • Yellow leaves indicate overwatering
  • Brown leaf tips suggest low humidity
