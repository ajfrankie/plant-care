Watering Your Plants

Master the most important aspect of plant care

💧 When to Water
  • Check soil moisture 1-2 inches below surface
  • Water when top inch feels dry to touch
  • Most plants prefer "soak and dry" method
  • Morning watering is generally best
  • Adjust frequency based on season
🚿 How to Water
  • Water soil directly, not leaves
  • Water until it drains from bottom
  • Empty drainage tray after 15 minutes
  • Use room temperature water
  • Avoid overwatering - #1 plant killer
📊 Water Needs by Type
  • High: Ferns, Peace Lily (weekly)
  • Medium: Pothos, Monstera (10 days)
  • Low: Snake Plant, ZZ Plant (2-3 weeks)
  • Very Low: Succulents, Cacti (monthly)
Pro Tip
The "finger test" is your best friend. Stick your finger 1-2 inches into the soil. If it's dry, water. If it's moist, wait. Over time, you'll develop a feel for each plant's needs.

Light Requirements

Understanding your plant's light needs

☀️
Bright Direct Light

Direct sunlight for 4-6 hours daily. South or west-facing windows.

Best for: Succulents, Cacti, Jade Plant, Aloe Vera, String of Pearls
🌤️
Bright Indirect Light

Bright room but not direct sun rays. East-facing windows or filtered south windows.

Best for: Monstera, Fiddle Leaf Fig, Rubber Plant, Philodendron, Pothos
⛅
Medium Light

Few feet away from windows. Can read comfortably without artificial light.

Best for: Snake Plant, Dracaena, Spider Plant, Peace Lily, Chinese Evergreen
🌙
Low Light

North-facing windows or interior rooms. May need artificial light.

Best for: ZZ Plant, Pothos, Cast Iron Plant, Snake Plant, Parlor Palm
Warning Signs
Too much light: Brown, crispy leaves; faded color. Too little light: Leggy growth, small leaves, leaning toward window.

Soil & Potting

Choose the right foundation for healthy growth

Soil Types
All-Purpose Potting Mix
Most houseplants, balanced drainage
Cactus/Succulent Mix
Fast-draining, sandy composition
Orchid Mix
Bark-based, excellent airflow
African Violet Mix
Light, fluffy, acidic pH
Pot Selection
  • Drainage holes: Essential for most plants
  • Size: 1-2 inches larger than root ball
  • Material: Terra cotta breathes, plastic retains moisture
  • Depth: Match root system depth
When to Repot
  • Roots growing through drainage holes
  • Water runs straight through pot
  • Plant becomes top-heavy, tips over
  • Growth has slowed significantly
  • Soil breaks down (every 1-2 years)

Fertilizing Guide

Feed your plants for optimal growth

Fertilizing Schedule
Growing Season (Spring/Summer) Every 2-4 weeks
Dormant Season (Fall/Winter) Monthly or none
Newly Repotted Plants Wait 4-6 weeks
Best Practices
  • Use balanced liquid fertilizer (10-10-10 or 20-20-20)
  • Dilute to half or quarter strength
  • Water before fertilizing to prevent root burn
  • Less is more - over-fertilizing damages plants
  • Flush soil monthly to prevent salt buildup
✂️

Pruning & Maintenance

Keep your plants shaped and healthy

When & What to Prune
✓ Always Remove
  • Dead, brown, or yellow leaves
  • Diseased or pest-damaged parts
  • Crossing or rubbing branches
  • Leggy or sparse growth
⏰ Best Time
  • Spring: major shaping and pruning
  • Growing season: pinch tips for bushiness
  • Anytime: remove dead/damaged parts
  • Avoid heavy pruning in winter
🔪 Tools Needed
  • Clean, sharp scissors or pruners
  • Rubbing alcohol for sterilizing
  • Clean cloth or paper towels
  • Gloves (for toxic plants)
Cleaning Leaves
  • Wipe dust with damp cloth monthly
  • Shower plants to remove buildup
  • Improves photosynthesis and appearance
  • Check for pests while cleaning
Staking & Support
  • Use moss poles for climbing plants
  • Bamboo stakes for tall, top-heavy plants
  • Soft ties to avoid damage
  • Train growth direction early

Plant Propagation

Multiply your plant collection for free

💧
Water Propagation

The easiest method for beginners. Watch roots grow in clear water.

Steps:
  1. Cut 4-6 inch stem below node
  2. Remove lower leaves
  3. Place in clean water
  4. Change water weekly
  5. Plant when roots are 2+ inches
Best for:
Pothos, Philodendron, Monstera, Spider Plant, Lucky Bamboo, Coleus, Begonia
🌱
Soil Propagation

Plant cuttings directly in soil. Higher success rate for some plants.

Steps:
  1. Take 4-6 inch cutting
  2. Optional: dip in rooting hormone
  3. Plant in moist potting mix
  4. Keep soil lightly moist
  5. Cover with plastic for humidity
Best for:
Succulents, Cacti, Fiddle Leaf Fig, Rubber Plant, Snake Plant, African Violets
👶
Division & Offsets

Split mature plants or separate baby plants (pups) from parent.

Steps:
  1. Remove plant from pot
  2. Gently separate root clumps
  3. Cut if needed with clean knife
  4. Plant divisions in new pots
  5. Water thoroughly
Best for:
Snake Plant, Spider Plant, Peace Lily, ZZ Plant, Aloe, Succulents, Ferns
Success Tips
Spring and early summer are the best times to propagate. Use clean tools to prevent disease. Be patient - roots can take 2-8 weeks to develop depending on the plant.

Common Pests & Diseases

Identify and treat plant problems

🕷️
Spider Mites
Signs:
Tiny webs, yellow speckles on leaves, fine webbing between stems
Treatment:
Spray with water, neem oil, insecticidal soap. Increase humidity. Isolate infected plants.
🦟
Fungus Gnats
Signs:
Small black flies around soil, larvae in soil, flying when watering
Treatment:
Let soil dry out between waterings. Yellow sticky traps. Mosquito bits in water. Top layer of sand.
🐚
Scale Insects
Signs:
Brown or white bumps on stems/leaves, sticky residue, yellowing leaves
Treatment:
Scrape off manually with cloth. Rubbing alcohol on cotton swab. Neem oil spray weekly.
🦗
Mealybugs
Signs:
White cottony masses in leaf joints, sticky leaves, stunted growth
Treatment:
Dab with rubbing alcohol. Spray with neem oil. Insecticidal soap. Repeat weekly for 3 weeks.
🍄
Root Rot
Signs:
Wilting despite wet soil, yellow leaves, mushy black roots, moldy smell
Treatment:
Remove from pot. Trim dead roots. Repot in fresh soil. Reduce watering. Improve drainage.
Prevention is Key
Quarantine new plants for 2 weeks. Inspect plants weekly. Keep leaves clean. Avoid overwatering. Maintain good air circulation. Healthy plants resist pests better.

Troubleshooting Guide

Quick solutions to common problems

Yellow Leaves
Possible Causes:
Overwatering, underwatering, nutrient deficiency, natural aging (lower leaves)
Solutions:
Check soil moisture. Adjust watering schedule. Fertilize if needed. Remove old leaves.
Brown Leaf Tips
Possible Causes:
Low humidity, over-fertilizing, fluoride in water, underwatering
Solutions:
Increase humidity. Use distilled water. Reduce fertilizer. Water more consistently.
Drooping/Wilting
Possible Causes:
Underwatering, overwatering, root rot, temperature shock, transplant stress
Solutions:
Check soil and roots. Water if dry. If wet, check for rot. Avoid temperature extremes.
Leggy Growth
Possible Causes:
Insufficient light, reaching for light source, needs pruning
Solutions:
Move to brighter location. Rotate plant regularly. Prune to encourage bushiness.
Brown Crispy Leaves
Possible Causes:
Too much direct sun, underwatering, low humidity, fertilizer burn
Solutions:
Move away from direct sun. Water more frequently. Mist leaves. Flush soil with water.
No New Growth
Possible Causes:
Dormant season, insufficient light, rootbound, nutrient deficiency
Solutions:
Wait for spring. Increase light. Check if needs repotting. Fertilize during growing season.
Leaves Falling Off
Possible Causes:
Temperature shock, overwatering, underwatering, pest infestation, transplant shock
Solutions:
Check for pests. Maintain consistent temperature. Adjust watering. Give time to acclimate.
