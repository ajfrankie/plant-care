About Us

We're on a mission to help everyone grow thriving plants

Plant Care was born from a simple belief: with the right guidance and tools, anyone can become a confident plant parent and create a greener, healthier home.

Our Story

Plant Care started in 2023 when a group of plant enthusiasts realized they were spending more time worrying about their plants than enjoying them. Between forgotten watering schedules, confusing care advice, and plants that seemed to wilt overnight, the joy of having greenery at home was getting lost.

We knew there had to be a better way. So we built Plant Care — a platform that takes the guesswork out of plant parenting. No more sticky notes on the fridge, no more generic advice that doesn't work for your specific plants, and no more guilt when life gets busy.

Today, Plant Care helps plant parents keep their collections thriving with personalized care schedules and practical, organic-first guidance. Whether you have one succulent or a hundred houseplants, we're here to help them all flourish.

What We Believe

The values that guide everything we do

Plants for Everyone

Whether you're a beginner or expert, everyone deserves to experience the joy and benefits of growing plants. We make plant care accessible to all.

Our care recommendations are based on horticultural research and real-world experience, not guesswork. We prioritize organic and sustainable practices.

Community First

We're building a supportive community where plant lovers can share experiences, learn from each other, and celebrate their green victories together.

Simplicity & Clarity

Plant care shouldn't be complicated. We cut through the noise to give you clear, actionable guidance that actually works in real life.

Environmental Impact

More plants mean cleaner air and healthier homes. We're committed to encouraging sustainable growing practices that benefit both people and planet.

Continuous Improvement

We're constantly learning and evolving. Your feedback helps us build better tools and provide more useful guidance every day.

How Plant Care Works

From sign-up to thriving plants in three simple steps

1

Create Your Plant Profile

Add each plant to your collection with a photo, nickname, and location. Tell us about its light conditions and where it lives — we'll handle the rest. You can add plants from our extensive library or create custom entries.

2

Get Personalized Care Plans

Based on each plant's species and your environment, we create custom care schedules. You'll receive watering reminders, fertilizing alerts, and seasonal care adjustments — all tailored to your specific plants and conditions.

3

Track Growth & Stay Healthy

Log care activities, track plant health over time, and access expert guides when issues arise. Watch your confidence grow as your plants thrive. Our dashboard shows you everything at a glance — what needs attention and what's doing great.
