About Us

We're on a mission to help everyone grow thriving plants

Plant Care was born from a simple belief: with the right guidance and tools, anyone can become a confident plant parent and create a greener, healthier home.

Our Story

Plant Care started in 2023 when a group of plant enthusiasts realized they were spending more time worrying about their plants than enjoying them. Between forgotten watering schedules, confusing care advice, and plants that seemed to wilt overnight, the joy of having greenery at home was getting lost.

We knew there had to be a better way. So we built Plant Care — a platform that takes the guesswork out of plant parenting. No more sticky notes on the fridge, no more generic advice that doesn't work for your specific plants, and no more guilt when life gets busy.

Today, we're proud to help thousands of plant parents keep their collections thriving with personalized care schedules, expert guidance, and a supportive community. Whether you have one succulent or a hundred houseplants, we're here to help them all flourish.

How Plant Care Works

From sign-up to thriving plants in three simple steps

1

Create Your Plant Profile

Add each plant to your collection with a photo, nickname, and location. Tell us about its light conditions and where it lives — we'll handle the rest. You can add plants from our extensive library or create custom entries.

2

Get Personalized Care Plans

Based on each plant's species and your environment, we create custom care schedules. You'll receive watering reminders, fertilizing alerts, and seasonal care adjustments — all tailored to your specific plants and conditions.

3
